man dumbterminal

    
The gestures and keys you already know, on the screen in your hand.

    

      

        
A real trackpad

        
Tap to click, two-finger tap or press and hold to right-click, scroll, and hold and move to drag. Or just tap where you want.

      

      

        
Mac keys and shortcuts

        
⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧, Esc and arrows above the keyboard, plus one-tap Spotlight, Mission Control and shortcuts of your own. Or bring a keyboard, mouse or Apple Pencil.

      

      

        
Mac gestures

        
Three fingers switch Spaces and open Mission Control. Four fingers switch apps.

      

      

        
Talk to type

        
Dictate and send the words to any app on your Mac. Speech is recognized on your device.

      

      

        
Wakes your Mac

        
Wake a sleeping Mac with one tap, or let Dumb Terminal wake the one you used last as it opens.

      

      

        
Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

        
“Wake my Mac.” Ask Siri to wake, check or open your Mac, or put it on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

      

      

        
A laptop on a foldable

        
Open a foldable iPhone and the desktop sits above a keyboard and trackpad, like a small laptop.

      

      

        
Every display

        
Pick the screen you want on a Mac with more than one, and share the clipboard if you like.

      

      

        
On the big screen

        
Connect a TV or monitor: your Mac fills it, and your device becomes the trackpad.

      

    

  


  

    

      
No account. No server.

      
Dumb Terminal is built on the Screen Sharing that Apple puts in every Mac, so there is nothing to install there and nothing to sign up for. The picture and your typing travel between your own devices, on your Wi-Fi or your own Tailscale network; none of it passes through computers run by Dumb Terminal. No ads, no tracking. Saved passwords stay on your device, behind Face ID if you like, and your list of Macs syncs only through your own iCloud.
