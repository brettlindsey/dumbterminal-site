Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Dumb Terminal collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no server of ours.

What stays with you

  • Your Macs, settings and saved passwords are kept on your device. Passwords stay in the iOS Keychain and are never synced or sent anywhere.
  • Your list of Macs, without passwords, syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud. We can't see it.
  • Your screen, typing and voice go only between your device and your Mac, on your own network or your own Tailscale network. Dictation is turned into text on your device.
  • If you turn on Share Clipboard (off by default), text you copy is sent to your Mac.
  • Apple handles purchases. We never see your payment details.

Other services

Apple (iCloud and the App Store) and Tailscale, if you use it, have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Delete a Mac in the app and it's removed from iCloud and your other devices too. Deleting the app removes everything on that device.

Children

Dumb Terminal isn't directed at children.

Changes and contact

Changes will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support [at] dumbterminal [dot] app
