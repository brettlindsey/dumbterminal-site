Support

Questions or trouble connecting? Email support [at] dumbterminal [dot] app.

Set up your Mac

On the Mac, open System Settings, then General, then Sharing, and turn on Screen Sharing. Click the ⓘ next to it and make sure your user is allowed to connect.

Can't find or connect to your Mac

  • Put your iPhone or iPad and the Mac on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Allow Dumb Terminal to use the local network in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Local Network.
  • If a connection hangs, turn Screen Sharing off and on again on the Mac.

Away from home

Dumb Terminal reaches your Mac away from home over Tailscale, a separate free app. In Dumb Terminal, open Settings and tap set up on the away line: it walks you through it. This needs Away Access, which comes with Root Access, a one-time in-app purchase with a free 7-day trial. Macs on your own network are always free. For Tailscale sign-in problems, see Tailscale's help.

Restore a purchase

Restore Purchases is in Dumb Terminal: open Settings and tap the price on the root access line.

Beta feedback

Testing a beta? Open Dumb Terminal in the TestFlight app and tap Send Beta Feedback.
