What's new

Recent beta builds on TestFlight. The build number is in Dumb Terminal's Settings, at the bottom.

November 22, 2026 · build 261122.1832

  • Every theme now has a light and a dark look; choose in Settings, Appearance.
  • Pick a home screen for each theme: Home and Away, List, Big Tiles, One Big Mac, Status Board, Recent Sessions, Command Bar or Dock.
  • Era screen: an optional, faint look of each theme's old display (scanlines, an 80s TV, a dot matrix…) over the whole app, your Mac's picture included.
  • On a foldable's outside screen, the receipt now hands over to the clock with a little animation in each theme's style.
  • Original is now the last theme in the list.
  • Some things are hidden on the outside screen. Each theme has its own. Sound on helps.

November 22, 2026 · build 261122.1749

  • Original is back as the first theme: Dumb Terminal exactly as it was, with the iOS keyboard.
  • On a foldable, the iOS keyboard no longer covers the laptop keyboard, and the trackpad and session menu match the theme.
  • Closed, a foldable's outside screen shows only the theme's clock and a reminder to open it.

November 22, 2026 · build 261122.1730

  • Every screen now speaks its theme: Settings, adding a Mac, the welcome, the away setup checklist, connection errors and the session menu.
  • A soft glow marks the one thing that matters most on each screen.

November 22, 2026 · build 261122.1712

  • Themes: Terminal (the new look), Retro Basic, BIOS Setup, Neon Grid and Pocket LCD, plus Clean, the original. Choose in Settings, Theme.
  • Each theme has its own pointer over your Mac's screen and its own keys.
  • On a foldable, opening it like a laptop gives you a full Mac keyboard above the trackpad.
  • Closing a foldable ends the session, and its outside screen types a little receipt of it before settling on a clock, in each theme's style.
  • Connecting shows each step as it happens.

November 22, 2026 · build 261122.1559

  • Swipe with three fingers to switch Spaces, up for Mission Control, down for App Exposé. Four fingers switch apps.
  • Dictate: tap the microphone above the keyboard, speak, and send the words to your Mac. Speech stays on your device.
  • Tap ⚡ above the keyboard for Spotlight, Mission Control, App Exposé, Undo, Redo and shortcuts of your own (Settings, Shortcut Keys).
  • Plug into a TV or monitor, or use AirPlay: your Mac fills the big screen and your device becomes the trackpad.
  • Trackpad Only, in the session menu, hides the picture when your Mac's own screen is in front of you.
  • Your saved Macs show up on all your devices through your own iCloud. Passwords stay where you saved them.
  • On iPad, Apple Pencil points exactly: hover to move, tap to click, draw to drag. Mouse clicks always work.
  • Text you copy on the Mac stays on this device instead of spreading to your other devices.
  • Macs reachable over Tailscale no longer look asleep on the home screen when you're away.
  • Away Access is included free while you test the beta.
  • The handle at the bottom of a session sits in the middle of every screen, including a foldable's outer screen.

November 22, 2026 · build 261122.1400

  • Away from home now works over your own Tailscale network: no Dumb Terminal account, nothing in between.
  • Dumb Terminal finds each Mac's Tailscale address by itself, and a three-step guide sets it all up.
  • The lock button locks your Mac, or types your password into its lock screen when you ask.

November 11, 2026 · build 261111.1602

  • Faster, smoother drawing of your Mac's screen.
  • Haptic clicks on iPhone, like a MacBook trackpad, and a keyboard that follows light and dark mode.
  • Widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Earlier in November

  • Siri and Shortcuts: wake, check or open a Mac by name.
  • Dumb Terminal can wake the Mac you used last as it opens.
  • A laptop layout on foldable iPhones, with the desktop above a trackpad.
