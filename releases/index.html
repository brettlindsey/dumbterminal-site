What's new

Beta builds on TestFlight. The build number is in Dumb Terminal's Settings, at the bottom.

November 22, 2026 · build 261122.2205

The first beta of Dumb Terminal.

  • Macs on your Wi-Fi show up by themselves. Tap one and you're in. Free on your own network.
  • Away from home over your own Tailscale network. Dumb Terminal finds each Mac's Tailscale address by itself, and Settings, Set Up Away From Home walks you through it once. Away Access is included while you test the beta.
  • A trackpad with Mac gestures: three fingers switch Spaces, up for Mission Control, down for App Exposé, and four fingers switch apps. Or choose Direct Touch and tap where you want.
  • Mac keys above the keyboard (⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧, esc, tab and arrows). Tap ⚡ for Spotlight, Mission Control, Undo and shortcuts of your own, or the microphone to dictate. Speech stays on your device.
  • Lock your Mac, or type your password into its lock screen, from the handle at the bottom of a session.
  • Plug into a TV or monitor, or use AirPlay: your Mac fills the big screen and your device becomes the trackpad. Trackpad Only hides the picture when your Mac's own screen is in front of you.
  • On a foldable, open it like a laptop for a full Mac keyboard above the trackpad. Closed, its outside screen shows a clock.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets open your Mac in one tap, and a Wake Mac control wakes it.
  • Your saved Macs show up on all your devices through your own iCloud. Passwords stay where you saved them.
  • Eight home screen layouts, light and dark, in Settings.
  • Some things are hidden.
